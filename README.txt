# Medication Tracker Android PWA

## Install on Android
1. Upload the contents of this folder to a static HTTPS host (for example, a GitHub Pages site or Netlify).
2. Open the resulting HTTPS website in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
3. Tap **Install app** if it appears, or Chrome menu (⋮) → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
4. Open it from the home screen. Checkmarks are saved locally on that device/browser.

## Important
- This is an installable web app (PWA), not a signed APK.
- It works offline after the first successful load.
- Checkmarks are stored in local browser storage. Clearing browser data or changing devices may erase them.
- No automatic medication alarms are included.
- The schedule is based on the details discussed: Furosemide 40 mg Friday/Sunday/Tuesday at 7 AM; Cefuroxime 500 mg at 8 AM and 8 PM for 7 days; Azithromycin 500 mg at 9 AM after a full meal for 7 days; Spironolactone 25 mg Saturday/Monday/Wednesday in the morning.
- Verify all doses and dates against the latest prescription before relying on this tracker. The Furosemide 40 mg dose came from the earlier schedule, while the dose was not restated in the latest message.
